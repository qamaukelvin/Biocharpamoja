Featured Projects

Explore some of our recent projects and see how we have combined technology, community training, and measurable outcomes.

Kisii & Nyakach RoCC Kiln Pilots

In collaboration with Kisii University, KMFRI, and the Ahero Irrigation Scheme, Biochar Pamoja test the first RoCC Kilns fabricated in Kenya.

PowerPellet TLUD Cookstoves

Clean cooking stoves that also produce biochar and electricity for lighting and mobile charging, empowering women’s groups in Bungoma.

Bungoma Biochar Farming

Training 200+ farmers in Bungoma to turn crop residues into biochar, boosting maize yields by 20–30% and generating verified carbon credits
