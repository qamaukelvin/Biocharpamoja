Our Projects

Every project is designed to combine technology, community training, and measurable outcomes. These initiatives not only strengthen farming systems in Kenya but also deliver verified climate solutions that inspire replication worldwide

Bungoma Biochar Farming Project (2022–Present)

Activities

  • Deployed Panel Kilns to farmer groups for maize residue conversion
  • Conducted the Bungoma Maize Experiment (2022) comparing biochar-treated vs untreated plots
  • Trained 200+ farmers in kiln operation, biochar application, and soil management

Results

  • Farmers saw 20–30% higher maize yields in biochar plots
  • Biochar improved water retention, helping crops withstand drought
  • Fertilizer use decreased, lowering farming costs

Impact

  • 42 tonnes of crop residues converted into biochar
  • 87 tonnes of biochar documented between 2021–2025
  • Verified 170+ tCO₂e removed via CharTrac MRV
  • Demonstrated that smallholder farming can unlock carbon credit revenue streams

The Panel Kiln

Description

Modular, portable flame-cap kilns made of steel panels. Designed for medium-scale farmer groups.

How It Works

Residues (maize stalks, husks, sugarcane trash, woody shrubs) are layered and burned under controlled low-oxygen conditions. Smoke is consumed by the flame curtain, producing biochar instead of ash

Benefits

  • Consistent, high-quality biochar
  • Portable and easy to set up
  • Yield impact: 25–30% maize increase

Climate Role

Produces ~25 tonnes of biochar per year (~50 tCO₂e removed)

Kisii & Nyakach RoCC Kiln Pilots (2021)

Activities

  • Fabricated the first RoCC kilns at Kisii University workshops
  • Tested with multiple feedstocks: soybean residues, rice straw, Acacia shrubs
  • Collaborated with Kisii University, KMFRI, and Ahero Irrigation Scheme

Results

  • Daily outputs ranged from 8–40 kg of biochar depending on feedstock
  • Efficient carbonization with reduced smoke and emissions

Impact

  • Proved potential for community-scale kilns
  • Lessons learned informed the design of the Panel Kiln, which is easier to transport and operate for farmer groups

ROCC Kiln (Retort-Optimized Cone Kiln)

Description

Hybrid kiln combining a cone shape with retort efficiency. Larger capacity for community-scale production

How It Works

Biomass is added in layers; a flame curtain burns off gases and smoke. The retort chamber ensures high carbonization efficiency

Benefits

  • Large batch capacity, suitable for cooperatives
  • Very low emissions compared to traditional burning
  • Bungoma trials: +0.5–1 tonne maize per acre

Climate Role

Produces ~25 tonnes of biochar per year (~50 tCO₂e removed)

PowerPellet TLUD Cookstoves (2023–Present)

Millions of rural households in Kenya still rely on smoky open fires or charcoal, leading to deforestation and health risks. The PowerPellet TLUD-ND stove was introduced in Bungoma to address cooking energy needs while producing biochar and electricity

Activities

  • Deployed 25 stoves in partnership with the Rotary Club
  • Worked with 5 women’s self-help groups (WSGs) to manage distribution and pellet sales
  • Provided training on stove use, char collection, and safety

Results

  • Each stove produces ~100 kg biochar/year = 220 kg CO₂e removed annually
  • Families benefit from smoke-free kitchens and reduced firewood costs
  • Women’s groups earn 10% margin on pellet distribution

Impact

  • Target of 800 stoves by 2025, unlocking ~$7,500/year from carbon credits
  • Clean cooking + energy access + biochar production in one solution

TLUD Cookstove

Description

Modular, portable flame-cap kilns made of steel panels. Designed for medium-scale farmer groups.

Technology

  • Runs on pelletized sugarcane bagasse from ACE Pellets
  • Produces biochar rich in fixed carbon (≈62%)
  • Equipped with a ThermoElectric Generator (TEG) producing 8W of power for phone charging and LED lights

How It Works

Household stoves that provide clean cooking while generating biochar as a byproduct

Benefits

  • Reduces indoor air pollution
  • Provides electricity in advanced models (e.g., PowerPellet TLUD with 8W ThermoElectric Generator)
  • Farmers collect 5–10 kg of biochar per week for soils

Climate Role

Each stove = 0.5–1 tCO₂e removed annually
